## Rationale 
- It could measure the user's spending habit. Lower ratio indicates conservative spending and strong savings ability. Higher ratio indicates an overspending habit.
- It could draw the scale of customers’ ability about how much credit they can safely handle.
- It shows the area distribution of different customs, which can help to describe the level of different area.
